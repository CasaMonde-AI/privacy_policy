CasaMonde Outreach

Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

The CasaMonde Outreach app (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is an internal tool provided to authorised field representatives of CasaMonde and its partner organisations. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who this applies to

The App is used by authorised sales and outreach staff who sign in with credentials issued by their organisation. It is not intended for, or directed at, the general public or children.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what the App needs to do its job:

  • Account & contact information — your name, email address and phone number, used to authenticate you and attribute your field work.
  • Location data, including background location — while you are signed in, the App records your device’s precise location to (a) confirm you are at a partner’s office when you check in, and (b) map the route you cover on your beat, including when the App is in the background or closed. Location is collected only while you are signed in, and you can stop tracking at any time.
  • Audio recordings — when you record a voice note against a meeting, your microphone captures that audio.
  • Photos — proof-of-visit photos you capture during a meeting.
  • Partner and lead details you enter — names, phone numbers and related business information about the channel partners and leads you meet.
  • Diagnostic and usage data — crash reports, performance metrics and basic analytics, collected through Google Firebase to keep the App stable and reliable.

3. How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and secure your account, including single-device sign-in.
  • To record and verify field visits, meetings and the routes you cover.
  • To manage channel-partner and lead relationships on behalf of your organisation.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the App’s reliability and performance.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to operate the service for your organisation and to meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the address below; account deletion is also available within the App.

5. Permissions the App uses

  • Location (including “Allow all the time”) — on-site check-in verification and beat-route tracking.
  • Microphone — recording meeting voice notes.
  • Camera and photos — capturing proof-of-visit photos.
  • Foreground service — to keep route tracking running reliably while you are on your beat.

You are shown a clear in-app explanation before these permissions are requested, and you may decline or revoke them in your device settings at any time (some features will then be unavailable).

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted transport, access controls and single-device sign-in — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data against unauthorised access.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is an internal workforce tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above, and continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@casamonde.ai.
